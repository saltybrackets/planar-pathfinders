#Character Survey: Mantis (temporary name?)

**Backstory:** 
Mantis was a gladiator renowned among the Thri-Kreen on the planet Coronatus. She was the Princess’ lover and close to the royal family. In order to promote the King’s son as the next ruler, Mantis was asked to lose to the Prince in a battle. Loyal to the King, Mantis accepted. However, at the climax of the battle, the Prince revealed that the plan was for him to kill Mantis, garnering more popularity for himself by winning the duel. Mantis struck back and killed the Prince in bloody fashion. Mantis confronted the King about the Prince’s intentions and the King revealed that it was his idea to have Mantis killed. Mantis tried to kill the King, but ultimately failed. Mantis underestimated the King, as he was much stronger than anticipated. Having sacrificed her pride for the kingdom by accepting the battle with the Prince, lost a battle to the King, killed her lover’s (the Princess) brother, and with the only other option being execution, Mantis fled the planet.

Coronatus, in the training grounds, gladiator arenas, and orphanage

**What would motivate your character to travel with the party on adventures?**
Mantis loves the thrill of battle, but also wants to become stronger and recruit fighters for her eventual return home to kill the King.
