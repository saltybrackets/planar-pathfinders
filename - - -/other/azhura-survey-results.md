- **Where did your character come from?**
Azhura comes from the crimson flame clan in the sword mountains, the patriarch of the clan Skaerth the tyrant reigns over a fairly bast territory where some of his offspring reside and are treated as his property, the clan follows a very strict hierarchy where Azhura as the youngest was at the bottom.

**What would motivate your character to travel with the party on adventures?**
Fear, for the most part Azhura is trying her best to escape her father's clutches. Her experiences first as a member of the clan and then as a cursed dragon and an excile make her fear for her life but also that of those around her. She is being hunted, and she knows it, so she tries to maintain a low profile; however, she knows that having good companions that can watch her back is the difference between life, death, or worse.
Furthermore, she knows that working with others is the only way to achieve her final goal, to break her curse and depose her father and the rest of the clan, thus fulfilling her oath of vengance to Bahamut

**Come up with one short-term, mid-term, and long-term goal.**
- Short Term: Escape her fathers agents that are hunting her, survive and maintain her freedom. Especially she thinks there may be a local Noble that is actually a secret agent of the cult of the dragon and a subordinate to her father.
- Mid Term: Find the platinum blade, a legendary weapon that was left by Bahamut on Faerun that is said can cut any and all curses, including divine ones. This is her main and possible only way to break her curse.
- Long Term: To depose her father and dismantele the crimson flame clan, to achieve her vengance against those that spread terror and cruelty across the sword coast.

Starting MacGuffin would be her pendant of Bahamut, it is her main way to communicate with the platinum dragon and receive his guidance in moments of deep distres
