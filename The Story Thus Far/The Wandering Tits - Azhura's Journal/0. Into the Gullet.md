### 12 Eleint, 1493 DR
Summer is coming to an end, and autumn will soon be upon us.

It has been some time since I last wrote here—about six months, I believe. Nearly as long as it has been since Mr. [[Tok Tok]] found me and nursed me back to health.

After the destruction of the Monastery of the Platinum Dawn, I was devastated and had nowhere left to go. That was when he offered me work at his tavern. I have lived and worked here ever since.

Life at [[The Wandering Tit]] was good.

My days were filled with enough tasks to keep my mind occupied. During the day, we cleaned the tavern and prepared the food and drink that would be served that evening. At night, I worked among the tables, bringing the customers their meals and ale.

The work has given me a new and considerably deeper appreciation for everything [[Harka]] once did for me.

During these months, I have also been blessed with new friends besides Mr. Tok Tok.

The first is [[Trixie]], a [[Half-Tabaxi]] who, I am told, has worked at the tavern since it opened. She performs for the crowd at night. I must admit that I have grown to appreciate the ability of mortals to weave stories into song.

Few among my kind would even bother to attempt such a thing. I can already hear [[Ashra]] declaring it beneath her, while [[Skaerth|Father]] would demand to know its practical value.

But I digress.

More recently, an insectoid woman named [[Mantis]] joined our little group. She belongs to a people I had never encountered before. I do not know the details of how she came here, and I have little desire to pry into either my companions’ pasts or Mr. Tok Tok’s decisions.

Regardless, she is a fine fighter.

I have sparred with her several times and remain surprised by how sturdy her body is, considering how thin her limbs appear. Granted, this judgment is based upon my current form. I cannot call it a new body anymore, but perhaps I would have held a different opinion before the curse.

In any case, I do not wish to spend too much ink recounting the past six months. Otherwise, I would need several additional entries, and given our present circumstances, that may prove difficult.

As for the events of today, everything began normally.

We followed our usual routine and shared a round of ale shortly before opening the tavern to the public. Until that moment, I had believed Mantis incapable of speech. We were therefore all surprised when we suddenly heard her speak... or, more precisely, when she communicated directly into our minds.

I had never experienced telepathy before. It was deeply strange.

Should I ever have another chance to speak with my big brother, I will have to compare my experience with what he encountered among the gith. I have heard that they possess psychic talents of their own.

Once we recovered from the surprise of discovering Mantis’s ability, we hurried to finish preparing the tavern. The evening proceeded normally for some time, aside from the usual collection of rude patrons.

One of them pulled my tail.

I have endured harassment before, but this was the first time one of those damned commoners dared lay his hands upon me in such a manner. I nearly roasted him alive. Had Mr. Tok Tok not intervened, I very well might have.

Even remembering it makes my blood boil.

For a mortal commoner to dare—

*Several lines of angry scribbling and deep scratches cut across the page.*

Enough.

I must focus before I lose the thread of the day’s events.

The evening continued according to its usual rhythm, and even Mr. Tok Tok performed for our patrons. His performances are quite different from Trixie’s. They combine music with physical acting, but rather than telling a story in the same manner as one of her songs, his movements seem intended to convey emotion.

This particular performance concerned a golem that wished to become a mortal of flesh and blood. It endured a number of strange adventures, including being swallowed by a whale, of all things.

When Mr. Tok Tok finished, it was Trixie’s turn.

She began as she normally does, but then her voice changed.

Her movements changed as well. She moved as though something else had taken hold of her body. The song she sang gave me goosebumps.

It still does.

I questioned her afterward, but she had no true memory of what had happened.

No sooner had the song ended than fire erupted around the tavern walls.

Blood-red fire.

My clan’s signature.

Two figures entered through the flames, both wearing cloaks of the same crimson hue, embroidered with golden thread. They appeared human, or at least like one of the other two-legged mortal peoples. There were no kobolds with them.

At first, I feared that Ashra or [[Infernia]] had found me—or worse, that Father himself had come.

The intruders gave me no useful answers. They knew who I was, but refused to reveal who had sent them. Whether they intended to capture me or kill me, I could not tell.

There were only two of them, and neither was accompanied by kobolds. That makes me suspect they were operatives stationed somewhere nearby rather than a force sent directly from the [[Sword Mountains]].

But that is only a suspicion.

They may have simply happened upon me. They may have been watching the tavern for days.

I do not know.

*Several tear stains spread across the page, thinning and smearing the ink.*

Once again, I have brought fire, death, and destruction upon those who offered me kindness.

Will my family ever allow me to live?

Or am I condemned to remain alone, lest I bring ruin upon everyone I dare to care for?

*The page has been crumpled, as though Azhura considered throwing it away before smoothing it out again.*

We fought the intruders.

One was killed. We dragged the other through a portal hidden in Mr. Tok Tok’s room. He told us that it led to a safe room.

When we emerged on the other side, we found ourselves in complete darkness. I truly miss being able to see in the dark.

Eventually, we managed to produce enough light to examine our surroundings. There was a door leading out of the room, and it stood partially open. This appeared to cause Mr. Tok Tok a great deal of concern.

We left the room to investigate.

I am still not certain how it happened, but Mr. Tok Tok lost the key to the portal. Then something appeared. I first thought it was a rodent, but that was not quite right.

It was something trying very hard to pretend that it was a rodent and failing miserably.

It had far too many legs.

The creature seized the key and began running across what I can only describe as cratered temple grounds. Mantis and Trixie attempted to catch it, but it slipped through their grasp.

The creature reached the far end of the grounds and ran toward a humanoid figure.

Then the space above us began to open.

I can describe it only as a vast maw—larger than the mouth of any living creature I have ever seen. I do not believe even Father’s jaws could compare.

As the maw opened, debris began falling from above and crashing into the temple grounds.

I suppose that explains the craters.

*The remaining lines dissolve into hurried, increasingly illegible scribbles.*
