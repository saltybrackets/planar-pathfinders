## Backstory
Azhura appears as a tall young woman with pale olive skin, a lean but athletic build, shoulder-length crimson hair, golden eyes with slit pupils, patches of red scales across her body, curved dragon horns, clawed hands, large draconic wings, and a long red tail. She is unmistakably neither dragon nor human.

Azhura comes from [[The Crimson Flame Clan]] in the Sword Mountains. The patriarch of the clan, [[Skaerth]] the Tyrant, reigns over a fairly vast territory where some of his offspring reside and are treated as his property. The clan follows a very strict hierarchy where Azhura, as the youngest, was at the bottom.

Azhura is trying her best to escape her father's clutches. Her experiences, first as a member of the clan and then as a cursed dragon exile, make her fear for her life, as well as the lives of those around her. She is being hunted, and she knows it, so she tries to maintain a low profile; however, she knows that having good companions that can watch her back is the difference between life, death, or worse.

## (DM Only) The Dark of It
Azhura was born into [[The Crimson Flame Clan]], one of the most feared red dragon clans of the Sword Mountains. She is the youngest daughter of [[Skaerth]], patriarch of the Crimson Flame, Inquisitor of [[Tiamat]], an ancient red wyrm whose tyranny has dominated the region for centuries.

Among dragons, Azhura is still very young. At only eighty years old, she is barely considered an adult, roughly equivalent to a human in her mid-twenties. Like all of Skaerth's children, she was raised to become another weapon of the clan, participating in raids against nearby settlements and taught from birth that strength, greed, and fear were the natural order of the world.

Yet those lessons never truly took root.

During one such raid, the Crimson Flame Clan attacked a secluded monastery dedicated to [[Bahamut]]. While searching through the ruins after the battle, Azhura discovered the monastery's scriptures. For the first time in her life, she learned that dragons were not bound to Tiamat's path alone. She secretly kept the amulet of a fallen priest and began praying to the Platinum Dragon in secret, hiding both her faith and her growing doubts from her family.

Her deception lasted less than a year.

When Skaerth discovered her betrayal, he saw it not simply as heresy, but as treason against both his bloodline and the Dragon Queen herself. Before the assembled clan, he branded her with Tiamat's mark across her back, invoking a powerful divine curse.

Rather than stripping away her draconic nature completely, the curse trapped her in a cruel middle ground. She was forced into the body of a mortal woman while retaining enough draconic features to be recognized as a monster wherever she went. Though she can still reclaim her true dragon form for brief periods, doing so comes at a terrible cost. Each transformation leaves her body ravaged by exhaustion, often pushing her to the brink of collapse. As she grows stronger, she has learned to endure the curse better, but it continues to exact its toll, and it will never truly fade until the curse itself is broken.

After the curse was complete, Skaerth exiled her from the Crimson Flame Clan. Hunted by kobolds and servants loyal to her father, Azhura fled the Sword Mountains with little more than scavenged clothing, a rusted longsword, and the amulet she had risked everything to protect.

Eventually, she found refuge at a temple of Bahamut. Though many distrusted her appearance, the amulet she carried and the sincerity of her faith earned her a chance to remain. There she spent several years learning to live among mortals, studying the teachings of Bahamut, training with the sword, and slowly overcoming the ignorance and arrogance instilled by her upbringing. She never stopped being a red dragon at heart—she remains proud, stubborn, and sometimes unintentionally condescending toward mortals—but she came to genuinely admire their courage, compassion, and willingness to stand together against impossible odds.

In time, she was accepted into [[The Order of Bahamut]] and swore the Oath of Vengeance, dedicating herself to hunting those who prey upon the innocent and ensuring that no tyrant would ever inflict upon others what her father had inflicted upon her.

She had finally found a place she could call home.

It did not last.

[[The Cult of the Dragon]], aided by agents of the Crimson Flame Clan, eventually discovered her whereabouts. They descended upon the town, burning it to the ground and slaughtering nearly everyone within. Azhura survived alongside only a handful of others, but once again she watched those she cared about perish because of her connection to Skaerth.

Believing that remaining in one place would only bring death to more innocent people, Azhura left for one of the larger cities farther from the [[Sword Mountains]]. There she chose to keep a low profile, taking work as a barmaid in a busy tavern. The job serves several purposes: it provides enough coin to survive in mortal society, allows her to remain relatively unnoticed, and places her at the crossroads of merchants, adventurers, bounty hunters, scholars, and travelers whose stories often contain fragments of forgotten history.

During the day she studies in libraries and temples, searching for any mention of ancient relics capable of breaking divine curses. By night she serves drinks, quietly listening for rumors that might lead her closer to her goal.

Her greatest lead is an artifact spoken of only in scattered legends: [[The Platinum Sword]], a holy relic of Bahamut said to possess the power to sever even curses laid by gods.

Until she finds it, Azhura continues to walk a dangerous path. The Cult of the Dragon still hunts her. Her father's spies remain active throughout the Sword Mountains. Somewhere beyond them all waits Skaerth himself, along with the family that once called her daughter before branding her a traitor.

She intends to see them again.

This time, she will not be alone.

- **[[Ashra]]:** Elder sister, she hates Azhura for her relation to Astaroth and it's the main one in charge of hunting her down.

- **[[Lord Halvern]]:** Member of the cult of the dragon and minion of Skaerth, he is somewhat obsessed with making Azhura his since it would be a way for him to get into the clan properly, not that the other dragons care about that but he makes for a useful minion to their schemes.

## (DM Only) Goals / Motivations
- **Short Term:** Escape her fathers agents that are hunting her, survive and maintain her freedom. Especially she thinks there may be a local noble that is actually a secret agent of the cult of the dragon and a subordinate to her father.
- **Mid Term:** Find the platinum blade, a legendary weapon that was left by Bahamut on Faerun that is said can cut any and all curses, including divine ones. This is her main and possible only way to break her curse.
- **Long Term:** To depose her father and dismantele the crimson flame clan, to achieve her vengance against those that spread terror and cruelty across the sword coast.
