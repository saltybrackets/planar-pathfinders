## Ideas
- Feywild's weird time properties could imply deeper connection to the Temporal Energy Plane. Then again, there's a fair number of demiplanes with weird time mechanics.
- Planar Geometries:
	- All the inner, outer, transitive, etc. planes exist according to the rules of Euclidean geometry.
	- [[The Far Realm]] exists according the rules of a non-Euclidean geometry, hyperbolic geometry.
	- The [[Temporal Energy Plane]] exists according to the rules of another non-Euclidean geometry, elliptic geometry.
	- The "planes" refer to these geometric concepts.

## Hooks / Adventures
- Players get dropped from a portal onto a mobile fortress in the middle of the Blood War in Avernus
- Adventure set in "The Loop" (and Undersigil location)
- Someone tries to release a contagion within Sigil. Duke Rowan is suspected, but he turns out to be the best ally. Clues eventually lead to a bioresearch facility in one of the upper planes. Paradise becomes a blodbath. Entire facility has a "self destruct" that drops it through an enormous Astral conduit to an apocalyptic Material world.

## Factions / Organizations
- Fractals, or "The Shrinkers": Believe all plane are actually one, an "overplane", and each plane is actually an infinitesimally small location within the overplane. Their proof is a portal that leads back into Sigil, but shrinks those who pass through it (this isn't at all  normal portal behavior).
