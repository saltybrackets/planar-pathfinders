Or "The 27th Order"
- Is trying to find, contact, or get to the [[Ordial Plane]], which they call the Noumenal.
- Believes the multiverse is ultimately made up of three things:
	- Substance (which travels by means of the [[Wiki/Locations/Planes/Transitive Planes/Ethereal Plane|Ethereal Plane]])
	- Belief (which travels by means of the [[Astral Plane]])
	- Proof (which travels by means of the [[Ordial Plane]])
- Speaks of the "Demiurge," a being who hides the Noumenal from the rest of reality.
- The number 27 is incredibly important to the order
	- 3 to the third power = 27
	- There are 27 planes total: 6 primary inner, 17 outer, 3 transitive, and the Material.
- Believes there is a being within the [[Temporal Energy Plane]] that may be able to provide access to the Ordial Plane.
- The [[Cult of Ouroboros]] splintered off of this organization
